# Handler Phone App

One-pager · Product, Dispatch · 8 September 2026

## Proposal
We should build a phone app for handlers. Right now the console is desktop-only, which means a handler is tied to their desk to see anything about their responder. A phone app would give handlers push notifications the moment something changes, a live view of their responder's current status, and one-tap contact straight from the notification — call or message, no need to open the console first.

## What this solves
Aunt Dot — a handler in the interview pile — asked for "maybe something that tells me too, not just him, when something's come in, so I'm not relying on hearing a buzz through a ceiling." A phone app would put that information in front of her the moment it happens, instead of whenever she next happens to hear it.

## Owner
The product designer, exploring for Q4 alongside the console work. Not yet staffed for a build.

## Success measure
Fewer handlers reporting they missed a status change because they weren't at the console. No baseline number yet — that's the metric to start tracking once this moves past exploration.

## Scope
Just the three things above: notifications, status view, one-tap contact. Nothing from Supply, nothing from the console's activity log — a phone app that mirrors the console's other features is a second project, not this one.
